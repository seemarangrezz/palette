Connection

Where Your Website Lives

A plain-English check of whether your site is saving to a shared database that works from any device — or only to this browser.

Checking…

    Connecting a database — about ten minutes, once

    1. Create a free account at supabase.com and click New project. Any name and region is fine; pick a database password and keep it somewhere safe.
    2. Set up the storage. In your new project, open SQL Editor, click New query, paste the script below, and press Run. It creates the content table and the photo bucket in one go.
    3. Create your login. Go to Authentication → Users → Add user, enter the email and password you want to sign in with here, and tick Auto Confirm User.
    4. Connect the site. Open Project Settings → API, copy the Project URL and the anon public key, and paste them into assets/js/config.js where it says SUPABASE_URL and SUPABASE_ANON_KEY. Save and upload the file to your host.
    5. Come back here and sign in with the email and password from step 3. Everything you upload now lives in the cloud, shared with every visitor and editable from any device.

    Publishing
    
        
    Your edits save automatically as a private draft. Nothing reaches the public site until you press Publish to Website at the top of the screen. If you change your mind before publishing, you can roll the draft back to whatever is currently live.
    
        
    
          
        
    
      
    

      
    
        
    Backups
    
        
    A backup is a single file containing every artwork, story and detail on your site. Download one before any big change — it's the fastest way to undo a mistake.
